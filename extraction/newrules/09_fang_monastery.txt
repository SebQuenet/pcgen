FEATS
The Fang Monastery teaches its disciples the
following feats.

Twin Fang Lunge (Combat)
You strike with the force of a serpent’s lunge.
   Prerequisites: Dex 15; Quick Draw; Twin Fang
Strike*; Twin Fang Style*; Two-Weapon Fighting;
Acrobatics 8 ranks; base attack bonus +8 or monk level 8th.
   Benefit: While using Twin Fang Style, you can spend a full-
round action to move up to twice your speed and then use
Twin Fang Strike as if you were taking the attack action.

Twin Fang Strike (Combat)
You strike at your enemies with both weapons simultaneously.
  Prerequisites: Dex 15; Quick Draw; Twin Fang Style*; Two-
Weapon Fighting; Acrobatics 6 ranks; base attack bonus +6 or
monk level 6th.
