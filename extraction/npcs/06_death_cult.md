# Death Cult — NPC Reference

Source: *Pathfinder RPG Villain Codex* pp. 66–77 (NPCs pp. 70–76).
This is reference documentation only (not loaded PCGen data). Reusable crunch
(template, feats, spells, equipment, magic items) from the group's *New Rules* is
inventoried in `extraction/crunch/06_death_cult.md`. Full numeric stat blocks are in
the PDF at the cited page; entries below summarise build, role, and notable mechanics.

New-rules elements used by these NPCs are marked `*` in the book: template *Pallid
Vector*; feats *Plague Resistance*, *Reap the Infirm*; spells *Outbreak*, *Virulent
Miasma*; gear *Pallid Suspension*; magic items *Plagueborn Mantle*, *Reaper's
Lantern* (*Urgathoa's Breath* is defined in New Rules but used by no stat block).

## Entries

### 1. Silent Needle — CR 4
Human rogue 5, NE. Lowest-echelon "subtle infection" ambusher and information
gatherer; sneak attack +3d6 plus bleed, high Stealth/Sleight of Hand/Disable Device.
Takes the *Reap the Infirm* feat and applies *Pallid Suspension* (plus *oil of magic
weapon*) to his rapier before combat. Uses Shadow Strike^APG and swift poison^APG
rogue talent. Page 70.

### 2. Ascetic Acolyte — CR 5
Half-elf monk 6, LE. Recruitment-focused "subtle infection" survivor of a virulent
disease (scarred/disfigured); flurry of blows, stunning fist (6/day, DC 15), Improved
Trip, immune to disease. Coats her shuriken with *Pallid Suspension* after buffing
with *bull's strength*, *mage armor*, and *oil of magic weapon*. Appears in the
Infectious Recruiters (CR 6) encounter. Page 70.

### 3. Bone Bastion — CR 5
Human fighter (phalanx soldier 6, *APG*), NE. "Death's embrace" front-line defender;
phalanx fighting and ready pike with a *+1 longspear*, Shield Focus and Saving
Shield^APG. Takes the *Reap the Infirm* feat and carries a *Pallid Suspension*.
Page 71.

### 4. Plague Angel — CR 7
Human antipaladin 8 (*APG*), CE. "Death's embrace" brute; channel negative energy
(4d6), smite good 3/day, touch of corruption 7/day, cruelties (diseased, sickened),
immune to disease. Takes the *Reap the Infirm* feat and applies *Pallid Suspension*
to her scythe. Prepares murderous command^UM and corruption resistance^APG. Appears
in the Death Bringers (CR 11) encounter. Page 71.

### 5. Awakened Voice — CR 5
Human cleric of Urgathoa 6, NE. "Soul speaker" spiritual leader of cult cells;
channel negative energy 3/day (DC 13), Death and War domains. Prepares the new spell
*Outbreak* (DC 17) and uses *Pallid Suspension* on her scythe. Page 72.

### 6. Death Chanter — CR 6
Human necromancer 7, NE. "Soul speaker" keeper of the Pallid Chant; grave touch,
animate dead, contagion (DC 20), Command Undead and power over undead. Buffs heavily
(*false life*, *mage armor*, *shield*, *spectral hand*) with a bonded *wand of
enervation*. No new-rules elements applied directly. Appears in the Death Bringers
(CR 11) encounter. Page 72.

### 7. Fevered Ravener — CR 6
Ghast slayer 4 (*Bestiary* 146, slayer *ACG* 53), CE undead. "Enlightened
plaguebringer" ghast assassin; bite/claws with disease and paralysis (DC 19), sneak
attack +1d6, studied target, stench aura. Carries five *Pallid Suspensions* and buffs
with *invisibility* and *greater magic fang*. Appears in the Diseased Assassins (CR 8)
encounter. Page 73.

### 8. Pestilent Messenger — CR 7
*Pallid vector* advanced minotaur (*Bestiary* 206), CE Large monstrous humanoid.
"Enlightened plaguebringer" albino minotaur bearing the pallid gift; the new *Pallid
Vector* template grants negative energy affinity, Pale Rebirth, stench, +3 natural
armor, immunity to disease/exhaustion/fatigue/poison/stunning, and disease-carrying
melee (*+1 greataxe* plus disease, powerful charge gore). Appears in The Inner Circle
(CR 13) encounter. Page 73.

### 9. Plaguebrewer — CR 9
Half-orc necromancer 10, CE. The Reaper's left hand and the cult's disease researcher;
CL 10th necromancy including the new spell *Virulent Miasma* (DC 20), plus *contagion*,
*enervation*, *cone of cold*, life sight, grave touch. Has the new feat *Plague
Resistance*, the SQ disease (pallid gift), and wears the *Plagueborn Mantle*; he
crafts the cult's *Pallid Suspensions*. Appears in The Inner Circle (CR 13) encounter.
Page 74.

### 10. Undead Apostle — CR 9
Dwarf graveknight fighter (two-handed fighter 8) (*Bestiary 3* 138, *APG* 108), NE
undead. The Reaper's newest lieutenant; sacrilegious aura, channel destruction (fire),
devastating blast (4d6 fire), undead mastery, rejuvenation, DR 10/magic, SR 20. Wields
the new magic item *Reaper's Lantern* (a *+1 heavy flail*) with Greater Weapon Focus
and Improved Critical (heavy flail); hunts clerics first. Furious Focus^APG. Page 75.

### 11. The Reaper — CR 10
Human ghost cleric of Urgathoa 9 (*Bestiary* 144), NE incorporeal undead. The cult's
ghostly founder and leader; corrupting touch (10d6), malevolence (DC 21), channel
negative energy 8/day (5d6), Death (Undead subdomain^APG) and War domains,
rejuvenation, SR 21. Prepares the new spell *Outbreak* (DC 18) alongside *slay living*,
*contagion*, and *enervation*; uses Sickening Spell^APG. Fights to the death, relying
on rejuvenation. Page 76.
