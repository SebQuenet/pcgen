# Merchant Caravan — NPC Reference

Source: *Pathfinder RPG Villain Codex* pp. 114–125 (New Rules pp. 116–117; NPCs pp. 118–124; Encounters p. 125).
This is reference documentation only (not loaded PCGen data). Reusable crunch
(feat, equipment, spell, magic items) from the group's *New Rules* section is
inventoried in `extraction/crunch/10_merchant_caravan.md`. Full numeric stat blocks
are in the PDF at the cited page; entries below summarise build, role, and notable
mechanics.

New-rules elements used by these NPCs are marked `*` in the book: feat *Craft
Shoddy Item*; gear *Sucker's Barrel*, *Smuggler's Wagon*, *Caravan Master's Wagon*;
spell *Fool's Gold*; magic items *Crate of Preservation*, *Unbalanced Scales*.

## Entries

### 1. Common Swindler — CR 1
Human rogue 2, NE. Fast-talking con artist who works marks with Bluff, Disguise,
and the occasional spell. Notable: sneak attack +1d6, evasion, rogue talents
(convincing lie [UC], honeyed words [APG]); Deceitful and Extra Rogue Talent [APG]
feats. Combat gear of consumables (oil of magic weapon, potions of mage armor,
invisibility, cure light wounds). Page 118.

### 2. Snake-Oil Seller — CR 3
Human cleric of Calistria 3 / rogue 1, CN. Peddler who mixes divine spells and
trickery; Charm and Trickery domains, channel positive energy, sneak attack +1d6.
Brews her own potions (Brew Potion). Uses Conceal Spell [UI]. Page 118.

### 3. Shoddy Artisan — CR 1
Dwarf rogue 2, NE (listed at Large because it is pre-buffed with enlarge person;
base size Medium). Substandard-goods maker who plays up his size to draw caravan
guards into a fight. Notable: uses the new *Craft Shoddy Item* feat to pass off
poorly built wares; Craft (armor/carpentry/locks/weapons), sneak attack +1d6. Page 119.

### 4. Crooked Retailer — CR 3
Human fighter 4, CN. Intimidation-focused merchant enforcing "all sales final"
policies; Enforcer [APG], Bludgeoner [UC], Intimidating Prowess, Power Attack, armor
training 1. Carries a *sucker's barrel* (new gear) plus a heavy wagon [UC] and
merchant's scale. Page 119.

### 5. Petty Fence — CR 2
Human rogue 3, NE. Buyer/seller of stolen goods; sneak attack +2d6, evasion, trap
sense +1, rogue talent black market connections [UC]. Escapes with potion of gaseous
form; Uncanny Alertness [UM]. Page 120.

### 6. Contraband Smuggler — CR 4
Halfling rogue 5, NE. Ranged skirmisher who lets thugs screen him; sneak attack
+3d6, uncanny dodge, evasion, many rogue talents (black market connections [UC],
convincing lie [UC], hard to fool [APG], honeyed words [APG]). Owns a *medium-quality
medium smuggler's wagon* (new gear); flees with potions of invisibility and
misdirection. Page 120.

### 7. Ruthless Moneylender — CR 2
Human illusionist 3, CN. Prefers to avoid combat and rely on guards; blinding ray
school power, prepared illusions (minor image, disguise self, magic aura). Carries
*unbalanced scales* (new magic item) and prepares the new spell *Fool's Gold*.
Opposition schools divination and necromancy. Page 121.

### 8. Corrupt Guild Member — CR 4
Half-elf rogue 5, NE. Coerces merchants into fake guilds; heavy social skill focus
(Skill Focus Bluff/Diplomacy/Sense Motive, Deceitful), sneak attack +3d6, rogue
talents (charmer [APG], honeyed words [APG]). Transports goods in a *crate of
preservation* (new magic item); ring of protection +1. Page 121.

### 9. Security Manager — CR 5
Human fighter 6, NE. Runs caravan protection; +1 battleaxe with weapon training
(axes), Power Attack, Shield Focus, Enforcer [APG], Bludgeoner [UC], Intimidating
Prowess, armor training 1. Front-line anchor while rogue allies flank. Page 122.

### 10. Face — CR 5
Elf bard 6, CN. Silver-tongued negotiator and front woman; bardic performance
(fascinate, suggestion, inspire courage/competence), broad enchantment/charm spell
list, circlet of persuasion, versatile performance (comedy, oratory). Avoids direct
combat and flees or charms out of danger. Page 123.

### 11. Caravan Master — CR 6
Human rogue (charlatan) 7 (*Pathfinder RPG Ultimate Combat* 72), NE. Owner and
master con artist; grand hoax, natural born liar, sneak attack +4d6, uncanny dodge,
Leadership, Master Craftsman. Drives the *caravan master's wagon* (new gear, with a
hidden portable hole) and carries a wand of nondetection and merchant's scale. Stays
out of the fray and flees from real threats. Page 124.
