# Fang Monastery — NPC Reference

Source: *Pathfinder RPG Villain Codex* pp. 102–113 (New Rules pp. 104–105; NPCs pp. 106–112; Encounters p. 113).
This is reference documentation only (not loaded PCGen data). Reusable crunch
(archetype, oracle mystery, oracle curse, feats) from the group's *New Rules* is
inventoried in `extraction/crunch/09_fang_monastery.md`. Full numeric stat blocks
are in the PDF at the cited page; entries below summarise build, role, and notable
mechanics.

New-rules elements used by these NPCs are marked `*` in the book: archetype *Hunting
Serpent* (ninja); oracle mystery *Ascetic* with its revelations; oracle curse *Toxic
Blood*; feats *Twin Fang Style*, *Twin Fang Strike*, *Twin Fang Lunge*. The *Ascetic*
mystery is already provided as loadable PCGen data.

## Entries

### 1. Fang Monastery Enforcer — CR 6
Human brawler (snakebite striker 7, *ACG*), LE. Bodyguard for infiltrators and
important agents; brawler's flurry, brawler's strike, close weapon mastery, knockout
1/day, sneak attack +2d6. Fights in tandem using the *Outflank* and *Precise Strike*
teamwork feats (*APG*); solo, feints and hunts lightly armored targets. No new-rules
elements. Page 106.

### 2. Fang Monastery Warden — CR 8
Human fighter 9, LE. First line of defense; a mobile two-dagger disarm build
(Improved Disarm, Disarming Strike *APG*, weapon training light blades/thrown). Uses
the new *Twin Fang Style*, *Twin Fang Strike*, and *Twin Fang Lunge* feats to strip
armor from disarmed opponents. Page 106.

### 3. Fang Monastery Infiltrator — CR 6
Human monk 7, LE. Stealthy operative; flurry of blows, stunning fist 7/day, evasion,
immune to disease. Enters *Snake Style* / *Snake Sidewind* (*UC*) and buffs with
*bull's strength* and *cat's grace* before combat. No new-rules elements. Page 107.

### 4. Fang Monastery Assassin — CR 7
Half-orc ninja (hunting serpent 8, *UC*), LE. Uses the new *Hunting Serpent* ninja
archetype: death mark (+2, 2/day) and relentless pursuit +2. Sneak attack +4d6 plus
4 bleed; applies *oil of keen edge* to a +1 wakizashi. Poison use, ki pool. Page 107.

### 5. Fang Monastery Sniper — CR 9
Human monk (zen archer 10, *APG*), LE. Ranged specialist with +2 longbow flurry,
zen archery, ki archery/ki arrows, reflexive shot. No new-rules elements. Page 108.

### 6. Fang Monastery Shadow — CR 10
Human ninja 11 (*UC*), LE. Two-weapon kusarigama fighter; sneak attack +6d6,
improved uncanny dodge, ninja tricks (distracting attack, feather fall, flurry of
stars, etc.), no trace +3, poison use. No new-rules elements. Page 108.

### 7. Fang Monastery Mystic — CR 8
Human monk (qinggong monk 9, *UM*), LE. Ki-fueled caster with qinggong spell-like
abilities (*barkskin*, *poison*, *scorching ray*); Snake Style / Snake Fang /
Snake Sidewind (*UC*); improved evasion, immune to disease. No new-rules elements.
Page 109.

### 8. Fang Monastery Ascendant — CR 10
Human sorcerer 11, LE. Serpentine-bloodline (*APG*) caster; serpent's fang,
serpentfriend, snakeskin resistance; poison- and snake-themed spell list (*poison*,
*fire snake*, *cloudkill*, *monstrous physique I*). Field officer for the masters.
No new-rules elements. Page 110.

### 9. Fang Monastery Master — CR 10
Human monk (sensei 11, *UC*), LE. Teacher/mastermind; advice (inspire competence/
courage/greatness), insightful strike, mystic wisdom, stunning fist 11/day. Uses the
new *Twin Fang Style*, *Twin Fang Strike*, and *Twin Fang Lunge* feats with paired
kama. Page 111.

### 10. The First Fang — CR 11
Human oracle 12 (*APG*), LE. Cult leader. Uses the new *Ascetic* oracle mystery
(mystery ascetic*) with revelations ascetic armor, martial discipline, oracular
spellstrike, and spell deflection, plus the new *Toxic Blood* oracle curse. Casts
*righteous might*/*divine power* and inflicts spells through unarmed strikes via
oracular spellstrike; torments foes with *bestow curse* and *disfiguring touch*.
Page 112.
