# Secret Society — NPC Reference

Source: *Pathfinder RPG Villain Codex* pp. 198–209 (New Rules pp. 200–201, NPCs pp. 202–208).
This is reference documentation only (not loaded PCGen data). Reusable crunch
(spell, occult rituals, magic items) from the group's *New Rules* is inventoried in
`extraction/crunch/17_secret_society.md`. Full numeric stat blocks are in the PDF at
the cited page; entries below summarise build, role, and notable mechanics.

New-rules elements used by these NPCs are marked `*` in the book: spell *Hobble*;
occult rituals *Cats and Mice*, *Enter the Inner Circle*; magic items *Animal Mask*,
*Society Ring*. The occult rituals build on *Occult Adventures* p. 208 (occult ritual
subsystem). Several NPCs use classes/options from other books (medium, occultist,
arcanist, summoner, mesmerist, spiritualist — all referenced, not new here).

## Entries

### 1. Rake — CR 6
Human aristocrat 1/rogue 6, CN. Dexterous cat-burglar and snatcher who abducts the
inner circle's targets; sneak attack +4d6, evasion, uncanny dodge, trap sense +2,
rogue talents (combat swipe [APG], finesse rogue, weapon training). Dual mwk kukris
and Improved Steal build. Carries an *Animal Mask* and a signet (mundane) ring. Page 202.

### 2. Cad — CR 7
Human fighter (cad 8, *UC*), CN. Dirty-fighting brawler specialising in dirty trick,
disarm, and steal maneuvers (deadly surprise, dirty maneuvers, payback). Carries an
*Animal Mask* and a signet ring. Page 202.

### 3. Channeler — CR 6
Human medium (relic channeler 7, *OA*), CE. Channels spirits of notorious dead
society members (archmage seance), casts arcane spells (fireball, haste, greater
oneiric horror [OA]), taboo (eschew divine power). Carries legendary spirit relics
(6) and an *Animal Mask*. Page 203.

### 4. Bearer — CR 7
Middle-aged human occultist 8 (*OA*), CN. Frail relic-guardian and spy; implement
schools (conjuration, divination, enchantment, illusion), masquerade, object reading,
misdirection. Uses a *Society Ring*. Page 204.

### 5. Caller — CR 9
Half-elf arcanist (occultist 10, *ACG*), LE. Summoner-specialist of the inner circle;
conjurer's focus, summon monster III–V, dimension door, augury/contact other plane
SLAs. Uses a *Society Ring*. Page 205.

### 6. High Sire — CR 11
Old half-elf summoner 12 (*APG*), NE. Master of the Hunt; commands the Great Black
Sphynx eidolon (maker's call, transposition, greater shield ally, life bond). Uses a
*Society Ring*. Page 206.

### 7. Great Black Sphynx — CR —
Quadruped eidolon, NE Large outsider. The society's sacred giant cat and focus of its
rites; pounce, rake, grab on bite and claws, DR 5/good, SR 23, resist fire 15. Bonded
to the High Sire. Page 206.

### 8. High Talon — CR 12
Old human mesmerist 13 (*OA*), LE. Master of the Lodge and public face/recruiter;
hypnotic and bold stares, mesmerist tricks, dominate/charm/possession spells,
consummate liar, glib lie. Carries an *Animal Mask* and a *Society Ring*. Page 207.

### 9. Grand Dam — CR 12
Young human spiritualist (haunted 13, *OA*), NE. Granddaughter and conduit of the
Grand Malkin; bonded phantom, possession/dominate/shadow spells, greater spiritual
interference. Uses a *Society Ring* and carries the *Book of the Black Sphynx* (a
unique tome, described in her stat block, granting +2 competence on occult ritual
skill checks — not in the New Rules section, so not inventoried as loadable crunch).
Page 208.

### 10. Grand Malkin — CR —
Jealousy phantom, NE Large outsider (phantom). The deceased Master of the Rites,
tethered to the Grand Dam; resentful aura, teleplasmic surge, usurp manifestation,
usurp spell, retribution, invisibility, DR 10/magic and slashing. Page 208.
