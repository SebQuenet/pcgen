(archetype, magic items) from the group's *New Rules* is inventoried in
`extraction/crunch/03_carnival_troupe.md`. Full numeric stat blocks are in the PDF
at the cited page; entries below summarise build, role, and notable mechanics.

*Fortune-Teller* (Bard); magic items *Aerialist's Rod*, *Fool's Malady*,
*Home Away*, *Mountebank's Megaphone*, *Powerhouse Pelt*. (*Home Away* is defined in
New Rules but is not carried by any NPC in this group.)

## Entries

### 1. Roustabout — CR 4
Human expert 3/warrior 3, CN. The all-purpose manual laborers who make up the bulk
of a traveling show — toting supplies, raising tents, standing guard, wrangling
beasts, and performing when needed. Tactics lean on feinting and demoralizing
(Catch Off-Guard, Persuasive, Power Attack, Toughness); they lull opponents with
feigned frailty. Carries a *Fool's Malady* ring. Page 34.

### 2. Strongwoman — CR 8
Human fighter 9, CN. A star performer who wrecks enemy advantages: sunders weapons,
grapples spellcasters (Greater/Improved Grapple, Improved Sunder, Vital Strike).
Wears a *Powerhouse Pelt* (+1 hide) and wields a +1 earth breaker^UE. Page 34.

### 3. Geek — CR 4
Human ogrekin barbarian 4 (*Bestiary 2* 204), NE. A raging sideshow brute with
oversized limb and deformed hand; throws itself into combat ignoring pain (animal
fury, no escape rage powers). Uses a Large mwk warhammer. Page 35.

### 4. Firebreather — CR 6
Human pyrokineticist 7 (*Occult Adventures* 10), NE. A kineticist posing as a master
of foreign fire magic; fire blast / blue flame blast, burning and fan-of-flames
infusions, searing flesh defense, elemental overflow. Page 35.

### 5. Acrobat — CR 5
Human rogue (acrobat 6, *APG* 132), CN. Aerial tumbler and knife-thrower who never
holds still, using expert leaper and second chance to rain daggers from beyond reach.
Carries an *Aerialist's Rod*. Rogue talents expert leaper^APG, fast stealth, stand up.
Page 36.

### 6. Clown — CR 7
Human slayer 8 (*Advanced Class Guide* 53), CE. A restrained force of chaos who
stalks prey from hiding and strikes with hit-and-run terror; sneak attack +2d6,
studied target, slayer talents (bleeding attack, foil scrutiny^ACG, slowing
strike^ACG, surprise attacks). Page 36.

### 7. Fortune-Teller — CR 7
Half-elf bard (fortune-teller* 8), NE. The archetype NPC: equal parts con artist and
mystic, using bardic performance and divinations to manipulate marks. Uses the
new *Fortune-Teller* archetype features — oracular performance, fortune-teller's
acumen, transparent fate (DC 18). Carries a *Mountebank's Megaphone*. Spymaster/
advisor rather than front-line combatant (leads from the rear with glibness and
pyrotechnics). Page 37.

### 8. Beast Trainer — CR 8
Human ranger 9, NE. Handles and fights alongside intimidating creatures; two-weapon
kukri fighter with favored enemy (animals, humans) and a constrictor snake animal
companion via hunter's bond. Prepares barkskin, charm animal, longstrider, speak
with animals. Page 38.

*Companion — Constrictor Snake (N Large animal, page 38):* CR —, the beast trainer's
hunter's-bond animal companion. Bite + grab/constrict grappler (CMB +18 grapple),
evasion, six taught tricks. Appears as an encounter pairing "Beast Trainer and
Constrictor Snake" (CR 8).

### 9. Stagecrafter — CR 9
Human illusionist 10, N. The quiet illusion specialist who fakes a carnival's
"wonders" — transforms bulls into minotaurs and jug bands into orchestras. Opposition
schools evocation/necromancy; arcane bond is a wand of shadow conjuration; casts
mind fog, greater invisibility, shadow evocation, phantasmal killer. Works best
unseen. Page 39.

### 10. Ringmaster — CR 10
Human mesmerist 11 (*Occult Adventures* 38), NE. The group's mastermind and only
figure of unquestioned authority; bends others to her will with hypnotic stare,
bold stare, painful stare, and mesmerist tricks, plus dominate person, phantasmal
killer, and oneiric horror^OA. Never fights alone. Page 40.
