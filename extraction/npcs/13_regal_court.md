# Regal Court — NPC Reference

Source: *Pathfinder RPG Villain Codex* pp. 150–161 (New Rules pp. 152–153, NPCs pp. 154–160).
This is reference documentation only (not loaded PCGen data). Reusable crunch
(archetype, magic item) from the group's *New Rules* is inventoried in
`extraction/crunch/13_regal_court.md`. Full numeric stat blocks are in the PDF at the
cited page; entries below summarise build, role, and notable mechanics.

New-rules elements used by these NPCs are marked `*` in the book: the witch archetype
*Mirror Witch* and the magic item *Ring of Rulership*, both carried by the Queen.
Superscripted elements (`APG`, `UC`, `UM`, `UI`, `ACG`, `ARG`, `OA`, `UE`) come from
other books and are noted only as NPC references, not captured as new.

## Entries

### 1. Crafty Courtier — CR 7
Half-elf bard 8, CN. A snooping courtier and informant who trades in secrets and
intrigue. Notable: bardic performance (22 rounds/day, move action; fascinate,
suggestion), enchantment- and deception-heavy spells known (*mad monkeys* [UM], *seek
thoughts* [APG], *triggered suggestion* [ACG], *focused scrutiny* [ACG], *honeyed
tongue* [APG], *adoration* [UC], *fumbletongue* [UM], *memory lapse* [APG]); feat
*Exile's Path* [ARG]; immune sleep, elf blood, versatile performances. Page 154.

### 2. Royal Guard — CR 8
Human fighter 9, N. Elite disciplined palace soldier; the court's enforcement arm.
Notable: weapon training (crossbows +1, heavy blades +2), armor training 2, shield
fighting; teamwork/martial feats *Intercept Charge* [ACG], *Outflank* [APG], *Pack
Attack* [UC], *Precise Strike* [APG], *Shield Wall* [APG], *Surge of Success* [ARG].
Page 154.

### 3. Captain of the Royal Guard — CR 10
Half-orc ranger (skirmisher 11, *APG*), LE. The true power behind the throne within the
guard; claims neutrality but favors the king. Notable: shield-bashing two-weapon combat
style, favored enemy (dwarves +2, elves +2, humans +6), quarry, hunter's tricks (aiding
attack, distracting attack, hateful attack, vengeance strike), favored terrain (urban);
feats *Outflank* [APG], *Shield Wall* [APG], Shield Master, Shield Slam. Page 155.

### 4. Petitioning Paladin — CR 9
Human paladin (divine defender 10, *APG*), LG. A righteous border lord seeking the
king's justice for a land dispute; a would-be ally of the PCs. Notable: smite evil
4/day, channel positive energy (5d6), lay on hands (5d6), divine bond (armor), auras of
courage/resolve/shared defense; spells *litany of righteousness* [UC], *righteous vigor*
[APG], *grace* [APG]; feats *Furious Focus* [APG], *Surge of Success* [ARG]. Page 156.

### 5. Elven Spy — CR 10
Elf ninja 11 (*UC*), CG. An enemy agent posing as an elegant courtier, gathering
intelligence for the elven holds and spurning the prince's advances. Notable: sneak
attack +6d6, ki pool (6), light steps, no trace +3, poison use, ninja tricks
(*assassinate* [UC], *master of disguise* [APG], *sudden disguise* [UC], *undetected
sabotage* [UC], *wall scramble* [UC]); Disguise +28; hat of disguise. Page 156.

### 6. Prince — CR 10
Human fighter 7/rogue 4, LE. The king's surviving son and nominal champion, with divided
loyalties between his father and imprisoned mother. Notable: sneak attack +2d6, weapon
training (light blades +1), armor training 3, rogue talents (*hard to fool* [APG],
weapon training), trapfinding, evasion, uncanny dodge; "bravery" feats *Improved
Bravery* [UI], *Inspiring Bravery* [UI], *Ready for Anything* [UI], *Social Bravery*
[UI]. Page 157.

### 7. Queen's Champion — CR 11
Dwarf fighter (unbreakable 12, *UC*), LN. A faithful bodyguard sworn to die before harm
comes to the queen. Notable: unflinching +3, defensive training, quick recovery,
*corrosive* [UE] warhammer, hatred; feats *Cleaving Finish* [UC], *Heroic Defiance*
[APG], *Heroic Recovery* [APG], *Ironhide* [APG], *Steel Soul* [APG]. Page 157.

### 8. Vizier — CR 12
Human mesmerist (vizier 13, *OA*/*UI*), NE. The sly manipulator lurking behind the
throne, embezzling and engineering the court's strife. Notable: hypnotic stare (–3),
painful stare (+6 or +4d6+6), bold stares (disorientation, sapped magic,
susceptibility), mesmerist tricks 12/day, glib lie (DC 28), insidious influence, power
behind the throne (DC 22), touch treatment (greater); many *OA*/*UI* enchantment spells
known (*foster hatred*, *possession*, *synaptic pulse*, *witness*, *deflect blame*,
*rumormonger*, *deja vu*, *mental block*); feats *Subtle Enchantments* [UI], *Third Eye*
[OA]. Page 158.

### 9. King — CR 13
Human rogue (rake 14, *APG*), NE. The charming, calculating, treacherous monarch who
favors parley over combat and strikes when the moment is right. Notable: sneak attack
+7d6, bravado's blade, evasion, improved uncanny dodge, rogue talents (*another day*
[APG], *assassinate* [UC], *charmer* [APG], *convincing lie* [UC], crippling strike,
finesse rogue, *redirect attack* [APG], weapon training); feats *Extra Rogue Talent*
[APG], *Fencing Grace* [UI]. Page 159.

### 10. Queen — CR 14
Human witch (mirror witch* 15, *APG* base class), NE. The imprisoned true power behind
the court, secretly free via mirror magic. Uses the new-rules archetype *Mirror Witch*
(patron mirror in place of a familiar) and carries the *Ring of Rulership* magic item.
Notable: patron mirror, see invisibility, immune divinations, hexes (cackle, charm, evil
eye, flight, misfortune, nightmares, slumber, vision, weather control), extensive witch
spells prepared (*mind blank*, *greater insect spies* [UI], *curse of the outcast* [UI],
*mage's decree* [UI], *major curse* [UM], quickened *ill omen* [APG], *witness* [UM],
*stricken heart* [ACG], *delusional pride* [UM]), *patron portents* [UM]; feats
*Accursed Hex* [UM], *Extra Hex* [APG], *Split Hex* [UM]. Page 160.
