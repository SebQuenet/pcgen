# Sinister Cult — NPC Reference

Source: *Pathfinder RPG Villain Codex* pp. 210–221 (NPCs pp. 214–220).
This is reference documentation only (not loaded PCGen data). Reusable crunch
(archetype, spell, magic items) from the group's *New Rules* is inventoried in
`extraction/crunch/18_sinister_cult.md`. Full numeric stat blocks are in the PDF at the
cited page; entries below summarise build, role, and notable mechanics.

New-rules elements used by these NPCs are marked `*` in the book: the *Voice of the Void*
medium archetype (used by the Cult Mystic), the *Hoodwink* spell (Cult Recruiter,
Sinister Mastermind), and the *Periapt of Devotion* magic item (Cult Cell Leader). The
minor artifact *Sigils of the Great Cataclysm* and the *Periapt of Utter Devotion* are
not carried by any stat block. Superscript tags (`APG`, `ACG`, `OA`, `UM`, `UC`, `UE`)
mark options drawn from other books and are noted per NPC.

## Entries

### 1. Cult Recruiter — CR 8
Human bard 9, NE. Charismatic proselytizer who lures and enthralls new converts through
performance and enchantment. Bardic performance (fascinate, suggestion, inspire
courage/greatness). Knows the new spell *Hoodwink** (2nd) to spirit victims away, plus
*charm person* and *suggestion*; Spell Focus (enchantment), Psychic Sensitivity [OA].
Carries a mask pendant. Page 214.

### 2. Cult Acquisitionist — CR 10
Human rogue 11, NE. Relic-thief who collects magical and symbolic objects for the cult.
High Stealth/Disable Device, sneak attack +6d6, improved evasion, rogue talents (canny
observer [APG], fast stealth, finesse rogue, quick disable). Casts *barkskin* from a wand
before combat. Psychic Sensitivity [OA]. Page 214.

### 3. Cult Thug — CR 9
Human rogue (thug 10, *APG*), NE. Enforcer who intimidates dissenters into line. Sneak
attack +5d6 plus 2 Str damage, brutal beating, frightening; Enforcer [APG], Intimidating
Prowess. Rogue talents include assault leader [APG], knock-out blow [APG], strong
impression [APG]. Carries an amulet of mighty fists +1 and a mask pendant. Page 215.

### 4. Cult Assassin — CR 11
Half-elf slayer 12 (*ACG*), NE. Silent killer wielding blade and crossbow against the
order's enemies. Studied target +3, sneak attack +4d6, crossbow combat style; slayer
talents (camouflage [APG], deadly range [UC], stealthy sniper [APG]); Clustered Shots
[UC], Deadly Aim. Uses poisons (scorpion venom, sassone leaf residue) and a *barkskin*
potion. Page 215.

### 5. Cult Mystic — CR 10
Human medium (voice of the void 11, *OA*), CE. Channels alien "emissaries" via the new
*Voice of the Void** archetype: emissary, blasphemy taboo, surge of the void +1d8, void
channeler 4/day (5d6, DC 19). Guardian spirit; summons and illusion spells (*phantasmal
killer*, *greater oneiric horror* [OA], *summon monster*). Page 216.

### 6. Cult Cell Leader — CR 11
Human ranger (urban ranger 12, *APG*), NE. Field commander of a cult cell in the city.
Favored enemy (humans +4), quarry, two-weapon combat style; blend in, favored community,
trapfinding. Carries a *Periapt of Devotion** (new item) to bind followers, plus a belt
of giant strength +2 and glamered agile breastplate [UE]. Page 216.

### 7. Cult Librarian — CR 9
Half-elf occultist 10 (*OA*), LE. Occult researcher guiding the cult's apocalyptic
agenda; may summon a cacodaemon for guidance. Implement schools (abjuration, conjuration,
divination, evocation, transmutation), mental focus 17, energy ray 5d6, aura sight.
Extensive spell list including *contact other plane*, *dimension door*, *fireball*.
Extra Mental Focus [OA]. Page 217.

### 8. Insane Prophet — CR 13
Human oracle 14 (*APG*), CE. Mouthpiece of the entity from beyond the stars, Dark
Tapestry mystery [UM]. Revelations: brain drain [UM], cloak of darkness [UM], many forms
[UM], pierce the veil [UM], read the tapestry [UM]; oracle's curse (tongues). Casts
*blasphemy*, *insanity*, *planar binding*, *feeblemind*. DR 10/adamantine. Psychic
Sensitivity [OA]. Page 218.

### 9. Daemonic Agent — CR 13
Human cleric of the Four Horsemen 14, NE. Liaison to the daemons of Abaddon; uses *planar
ally*/*planar binding* to call leukodaemons or piscodaemons as bodyguards. Domains Evil
(Daemon subdomain [APG]) and Death; channel negative energy 6/day (7d6). Carries a clay
figurine prepared with *refuge* and a silver unholy symbol of the Four Horsemen. Page 219.

### 10. Sinister Mastermind — CR 14
Human mesmerist (cult master 15, *OA*), NE. The cult's true leader; bends followers to
his will and seeks to merge with the coming apocalypse. Bold stares, hypnotic stare (–3),
mesmerist tricks 12/day, glib lie DC 30, fanatical devotion. Knows the new spell
*Hoodwink** (2nd). Leadership: cohort is a brutal warlord (*NPC Codex* 88), with pilgrim,
noble scions, doomsayers, farmers, and foot soldiers as followers (*GameMastery Guide*).
Page 220.
