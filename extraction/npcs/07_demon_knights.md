`villain_codex` source LST files. Full numeric stat blocks are in the PDF at the
cited page; entries below summarise build, role, and notable mechanics.

## Entries

### 1. Schir Inductee — CR 8
Human fighter 9, CE. The most common rank-and-file demon knight, emulating the
low-ranking schir demon. Halberd-focused polearm build (weapon training, Greater
Weapon Focus, Weapon Specialization, Lunge, Power Attack). Fresh recruits used as
expendable front-line troops. Page 82.

### 2. Abrikandilu Sunderer — CR 10
Dwarf barbarian (breaker 11, *APG*), CE Large. Modeled on the wrecking abrikandilu
demon; destruction-focused rager using Greater/Improved Sunder and rage powers
(abyssal blood/abyssal blood, lesser [*ACG*], ground breaker/smasher [*APG*],
strength surge). Wields a +1 greataxe. Page 82.

### 3. Babau Poisoner — CR 8
Human slayer 9 (*ACG*), CE. A demonic hunter emulating the babau. Sneak attack,
studied target, and slayer talents (bleeding attack, lasting poison [*APG*], poison
use [*ACG*], swift poison [*APG*]); carries blue whinnis, deathblade, and shadow
essence poisons. Page 83.

### 4. Nabasu Tracker — CR 11
Human ranger 12, CE. Relentless pursuer of those who flee the order. Two-handed
combat style (*APG*), favored enemy (humans +6), quarry, prepared ranger spells
including instant enemy (*APG*), brow gasher (*UC*), and snare. Wields a +1 keen
bastard sword. Page 83.

### 5. Babau Murderer — CR 9
Human rogue (knife master 10, *UC*), CE. Infiltration specialist; blade sense,
sneak attack +5d6 with +2 Str damage, and rogue talents (crippling strike, fast
stealth, fast tumble [*APG*], finesse rogue, surprise attacks). Dagger fighter with
glamered chain shirt. Page 84.

### 6. Shadowcaster — CR 12
Human illusionist 13, CE. Shadow-demon-inspired arcane leader of infiltration
squads. Casts the group's new penumbral spells: *Cloak of Shadows*, *Shadow
Jaunt*, and *Shadow Claws* alongside shadow conjuration/evocation and phantasmal
killer; illusion school (shadow subschool, *APG*). Binding darkness special attack,
staff of fire. Page 84.

### 7. Marilith Whirlwind — CR 11
Elf fighter 12, CE. Two-weapon melee whirlwind emulating the six-armed marilith.
Dual +1 longswords with Improved Two-Weapon Fighting, Greater Weapon Focus/
Specialization, and heavy-blade weapon training. Page 85.

### 8. Glabrezu Outrider — CR 13
Human cavalier 14 (*APG*), CE. Mounted commander (order of the cockatrice) using
challenge, banner, tactician/greater tactician (granting Outflank [*APG*] and
Tandem Trip [*UC*]), and Spirited Charge with a +2 lance. Hit-and-run mounted
tactics. Page 85.

### 9. Vrock Ritualist — CR 13
Half-orc cleric of Lamashtu 14, CE. Battlefield commander and strategist
administering recruits' oaths. Chaos and Evil domains (Demon subdomain, *APG*),
channel negative energy, chaos blade, scythe of evil; supports allies with rage and
dispel good while assailing foes with blindness and chaos hammer. Page 86.

### 10. Succubus Recruiter — CR 10
Human skald 11 (*ACG*), CE. Silver-tongued envoy who corrupts and recruits
warriors. Raging song (inspired rage, dirge of doom, song of marching/strength),
spell kenning, and enchantment spells (dominate person, charm monster, deep
slumber). Ranged +1 composite longbow. May employ the *Ivory Succubus* statue to
sway targets. Page 87.

### 11. Merciless Balor — CR 14
Human antipaladin 15 (*APG*), CE. The mysterious leader of the demon knights.
Balor-emulating whip-and-sword fighter using the new feats *Balor Whip* and
*Improved Balor Whip* (Quick Drag [*UC*], Whip Mastery [*UC*]) and wielding the
*Balor's Lash* magic whip. Antipaladin auras, cruelties, touch of corruption,
smite good, and prepared spells including unholy sword (*UM*). Page 88.
