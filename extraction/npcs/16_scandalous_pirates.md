# Scandalous Pirates — NPC Reference

Source: *Pathfinder RPG Villain Codex* pp. 186–197 (New Rules pp. 188–189, NPCs pp. 190–196).
This is reference documentation only (not loaded PCGen data). The reusable crunch
(feats, spells, weapons, magic items) from the group's *New Rules* is catalogued in
the companion inventory `extraction/crunch/16_scandalous_pirates.md`. Full numeric
stat blocks are in the PDF at the cited page; entries below summarise build, role,
and notable mechanics.

Strength*, *Improved Position of Strength*; spells *Cursed Treasure*, *Rotgut*,
*Walk the Plank*; weapons *Cutlass*, *Hook Hand*; magic items *Cutlass of Waves*,
*Hat of the Seven Winds*, *Pirate Lord's Patch*.

## Entries

### 1. Pirate Thug — CR 4
Human fighter 5, CE. Front-line boarder and the least of the pirates' ranks; prefers
to intimidate foes into surrender so captives can be sold as slaves. Uses the new
*Position of Strength* feat together with Improved Disarm to pressure disarmed foes,
cutting down those who refuse with Power Attack. Armed with a mwk *cutlass* (new
weapon). Weapon training (heavy blades +1). Page 190.

### 2. Drunken Brute — CR 6
Human barbarian 2 / brawler 5 (*ACG*), CE. Raging drunk melee bruiser; brawler's
flurry, knockout, martial flexibility, rage power good for what ails you (*APG*).
Almost always intoxicated, which imposes the sickened condition. No new-rules pirate
element. Page 190.

### 3. Tengu Sneak — CR 4
Tengu rogue 5 (*Bestiary* 263), CE. Stealthy finesse skirmisher; sneak attack +3d6,
rogue talents (fast stealth, finesse rogue), Tengu Wings (*ARG*). No new-rules
pirate element. Page 191.

### 4. Swift-Talking Scoundrel — CR 6
Human bard (daredevil) 7 (*UC*), NE. Charismatic support caster with bardic
performance and enchantments; casts the new *Rotgut* spell (bard 2). Page 191.

### 5. Swaggering Shot — CR 5
Human gunslinger (pistolero) 6 (*UC*), CE. Twin-pistol specialist using Two-Weapon
Fighting and Deadly Aim; deeds, grit, pistol training, alchemical cartridges (*UC*).
No new-rules pirate element. Page 192.

### 6. Musket Marauder — CR 7
Human rogue 8, NE. Long-range ambusher preferring a +1 musket (*UC*) with Deadly Aim;
sneak attack +4d6, rogue talents (firearm training *UC*, sniper's eye *APG*). No
new-rules pirate element. Page 192.

### 7. Sea Witch — CR 7
Human witch (sea witch) 8 (*APG*, *UM*), CE. Water-patron witch; hexes (cackle, evil
eye, flight *APG*, water lung *UM*), prepares the new *Cursed Treasure* spell (witch
3). Page 193.

### 8. Ship's Surgeon — CR 8
Human alchemist (vivisectionist) 9 (*APG*, *UM*), CE. Sneak-attacking extract caster;
mutagen, discoveries (alchemical zombie *UM*, bleeding attack, infusion), torturous
transformation. No new-rules pirate element. Page 193.

### 9. Daring Captain — CR 8
Human ranger 9, NE. Two-weapon captain of the *Amaranth Tide*; sunder-focused with
Greater Sunder, favored enemy (elves/humans), favored terrain (urban/water), lead
blades (*APG*). No new-rules pirate element. Page 194.

### 10. Ghost Captain — CR 9
Human ghost psychic 8 (*Bestiary* 144, *OA* 60), NE. Incorporeal undead commander of
the *Maelstrom*; psychic spells, phrenic amplifications, sailor's dread (Su,
phantasmal killer-like DC 16). Wears the new *Pirate Lord's Patch*. Page 195.

### 11. Vile Admiral — CR 10
Human swashbuckler (picaroon) 11 (*ACG*), CE. Supreme leader of the scandalous
pirates; pistol-and-cutlass duelist with deeds, panache, charmed life. Wields the new
*Cutlass of Waves* and wears the new *Hat of the Seven Winds*. Page 196.
