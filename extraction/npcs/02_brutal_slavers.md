## Entries

### 1. Beater — CR 2
Human rogue (thug 3, *APG*), NE. Slave catcher who softens captives with
nonlethal blows. Notable: brutal beating, frightening, sneak attack +2d6;
Enforcer (*APG*) and Sap Adept (*UC*) feats; drinks a potion of invisibility to
set up flanking sneak attacks. Page 22.

### 2. Enchantress — CR 2
Gnome sorcerer 3 (fey bloodline), CE. Slave catcher and battlefield disabler
using *charm person*, *color spray*, and *entangle*; laughing touch bloodline
power. Carries a scroll of *hide bruises* to conceal captives' injuries. Page 22.

### 3. Sadistic Healer — CR 3
Dwarf cleric of Zon-Kuthon 4, LE. Slave keeper who keeps slaves and slavers
alive while inflicting suffering; channel negative energy, Darkness (Loss, *APG*)
and Evil (Devil, *APG*) domains, *murderous command* (*UM*). Prepares *resist
starvation* to keep starving captives able to work. Page 23.

### 4. Sentinel — CR 3
Half-orc monk 4, LE. Slave keeper who blocks escape routes; flurry of blows,
stunning fist, Panther Style (*UC*), and a key of lock jamming (*APG*) to seal
exits. Page 23.

### 5. Tactician — CR 4
Human cavalier 5 (*APG*), LE. Mounted commander belonging to the new *order of
the whip*; grants allies Shake It Off (*UC*), opens with Improved Dirty Trick
(*APG*), and wields a mwk whip with Whip Mastery (*UC*). Fights from a Horse
animal-companion mount (CR —, statted alongside on page 24). Page 24.

### 6. Guide — CR 6
Human ranger (guide 7, *APG*), LE. Slave trafficker handling overland transport;
uses blunt arrows (*APG*) for nonlethal captures alongside new *darkness arrows*,
*vine arrows*, and *gags of silence* to blind, entangle, and silence prisoners.
Page 25.

### 7. Slaver Captain — CR 6
Elf swashbuckler 7 (*ACG*), CE. Slave trafficker who moves captives by river and
sea; panache deeds, +1 rapier with precise strike, Fencing Grace (*UI*). Page 25.

### 8. Bludgeoner — CR 4
Hobgoblin fighter 5 (*Bestiary*), NE. Slaver warlord running daily camp
operations through fear; Bludgeoner (*UC*), weapon training (hammers), Skill
Focus (Intimidate). Page 26.

### 9. Crusher — CR 7
Orc barbarian 8 (*Bestiary*), CE. Slaver warlord and raging enforcer; rage powers
(fiend totem and lesser fiend totem, *APG*, superstition), +1 heavy flail,
DR 1/—. Page 26.

### 10. Experimenter — CR 5
Human alchemist (vivisectionist 6, *APG*/*UM*), LE. Sadistic vivisectionist and
logistics leader; sneak attack +3d6, feral mutagen (bite/claws), wings and
infusion discoveries, bottled ooze (*UM*). Page 27.

### 11. Slave Master — CR 8
Human bard (court bard 9, *APG*), LE. The organization's eloquent face;
enchantment-focused performances (suggestion, glorious epic), heraldic expertise,
*cacophonous call* (*APG*), hand of the mage. Page 28.
