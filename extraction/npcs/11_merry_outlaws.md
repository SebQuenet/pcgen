## Entries

### 1. Merry Minstrel — CR 1
Half-elf bard 2, N. A talented troubadour recruited to gloss over the outlaws'
misdeeds with propaganda songs. Bardic performance (countersong, distraction,
fascinate, inspire courage +1), Skill Focus (Perform [sing]), versatile
performance (sing). Support/face role. Page 130.

### 2. Halfling Impostor — CR 4
Halfling swashbuckler (daring infiltrator 5, *ACG*), N. Poses as a fresh-faced
innocent to lure marks into ambushes; deeds (derring-do, clandestine expertise,
silence is golden), precise strike, Slashing Grace (*ACG*), disguise kit,
tear-away clothing, signet rings from minor noble houses. Deception specialist.
Page 130.

### 3. Woodsman Outlaw — CR 2
Human hunter 3 (*ACG*), N. Woodland skirmisher with a hawk animal companion;
Coordinated Shot (*ACG*), archery feats, animal focus, casts *gravity bow*
before combat. Ranged/nature role. Companion: Hawk (CR —, page 131). Page 131.

### 4. Deserter — CR 3
Human fighter 4, N. Ex-soldier turned bandit; two-weapon/shield-bash build
(Double Slice, Improved Shield Bash, Two-Weapon Fighting), armor training 1.
Notable: takes the *Cunning* feat for extra skill points. Page 131.

### 5. Ambushing Brigand — CR 1
Human rogue 2, N. Rank-and-file ambusher; sneak attack +1d6, evasion, rogue
talent (surprise attacks), trapfinding. Carries a *breathing tube* for
underwater concealment during ambushes. Page 132.

### 6. Outlaw Archer — CR 2
Human fighter 3, N. Peasant-turned-bowman covering ambush lines; Deadly Aim,
Point-Blank Shot, Precise Shot, Rapid Shot, Weapon Focus (longbow), armor
training 1. Ranged specialist. Page 132.

### 7. Halfling Filcher — CR 3
Halfling rogue (filcher 4, *ARG*), N. Thief specialized in stealing on the move;
sneak attack +2d6, Improved Steal, filch, quicker than the eye, rummage,
rogue talents (combat trick, finesse rogue). Pickpocket/skirmisher. Page 133.

### 8. Elite Marauder — CR 4
Human ranger 5, N. Raid organizer with archery/two-weapon flexibility; favored
enemy (animals, humans), hunter's bond (horse), casts *gravity bow* and *resist
energy*. Notable: takes the *Quick Stow* feat to swap bow and blade freely.
Companion: Horse (CR —, page 133). Page 133.

### 9. Outlaw Lieutenant — CR 5
Human ranger 6, N. The organization's sole lieutenant and second-in-command; a
strong quarterstaff fighter with two-weapon combat style, favored enemy
(animals, humans), *lead blades* and *longstrider*, +1 quarterstaff. Commander
and drill leader. Page 134.

### 10. Rebel Friar — CR 5
Half-orc cleric (separatist 6, *UM*) of Norgorber, N. Enigmatic divine support
and secrets-diviner; channel negative energy, Community and Trickery domains
(copycat, calming touch), Channel Smite, wand of cure light wounds. Founding
member and spiritual anchor. Page 135.

### 11. The Hood — CR 6
Human ranger (hooded champion, skirmisher 7, *ACG*/*APG*), CN. The masterful
archer leader; combat style (archery), deeds (dead aim, derring-do, kip-up),
panache, hunter's tricks (chameleon step, tangling attack), Manyshot, Point-Blank
Master (*APG*). Carries an *arrow of latching* and a *hood's flair* (black
feather token). Page 136.
