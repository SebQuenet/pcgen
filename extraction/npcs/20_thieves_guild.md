Source: *Pathfinder RPG Villain Codex* pp. 234–245 (New Rules pp. 236–237; NPCs pp. 238–244).
This is reference documentation only (not loaded PCGen data). Reusable crunch (feats, spells,
equipment, magic items) from the group's *New Rules* is inventoried in
`extraction/crunch/20_thieves_guild.md`. Full numeric stat blocks are in the PDF at the cited
page; entries below summarise build, role, and notable mechanics.

*Geomessage*; gear *Loaded Shell Game Cups*, *Quick-Pack Table*; magic items *Doorknobs of

## Entries

### 1. Guttersnipe — CR 2
Halfling rogue (cutpurse 3, *APG*), N. Foot soldier of the guild who pretends to be a beggar
and keeps tabs on the comings and goings of a neighborhood. Notable: finesse rapier build with
*stab and grab*, sneak attack +2d6, evasion, "measure the mark," and the finesse rogue talent;
Fencing Grace (*UI*) feat. Avoids combat, fleeing with a *potion of gaseous form*. Page 238.

### 2. Street-Corner Punk — CR 4
Human rogue 5, NE. Petty scammer and small-time robber who works only with the guild's
permission. Notable: Dex-based finesse dagger, sneak attack +3d6, evasion/uncanny dodge/trap
sense, rogue talents (charmer, fast fingers, honeyed words — all *APG*); buffs with *potion of
cat's grace* and *potion of aid*. Carries the new gear *Loaded Shell Game Cups* and a
*Quick-Pack Table*. Page 238.

### 3. Wiseguy — CR 2
Human fighter 3, NE. Middle-agent enforcer ("racketeer") who seeks out the strongest melee foe.
Notable: Str bruiser with mwk greatclub and brass knuckles, Power Attack, Intimidating Prowess,
armor training 1; buffs with a *potion of bull's strength*. Usually travels with street thugs
(*GameMastery Guide* 265) as backup. Page 239.

### 4. Gambler — CR 4
Human rogue 5, CN. Racketeer who hangs around taprooms fleecing marks at cards. Notable: sneak
attack +3d6, rogue talents (fast fingers, hard to fool — both *APG*), Skill Focus (Bluff) and
Skill Focus (Sleight of Hand); relies on *potion of invisibility*, marked cards (*UE*), and
superior loaded dice (*UE*). Page 239.

### 5. Second-Story Thief — CR 3
Half-elf rogue (heister 4, *UI*), CN. Member of a "night crew" of expert burglars; excels at
hiding, climbing, and lock-picking without killing. Notable: Disable Device +20, ferret's grace
and rum dubber (*UI*), sneak attack +2d6; buffs with *potion of cat's grace*. Wears the new
*Slippers of Scampering* magic item. Page 240.

### 6. Safecracker — CR 5
Human alchemist (vaultbreaker 6, *new archetype*), NE. Night-crew specialist who breaks into
vaults with focused charges. Notable: uses the new archetype *Vaultbreaker* and its bonus
discoveries — the new *Penetrating Charge* and *Incendiary Charge* — alongside acid bomb,
infusion, and precise bombs; Master Alchemist (*APG*), swift alchemy, Dexterity mutagen. Page
240.

### 7. Sleuth — CR 3
Human rogue (snoop 4, *UI*), N. Guild spy who digs up information through contacts and stealthy
access to restricted libraries and correspondence. Notable: inspiration (5), investigator talent
(underworld inspiration), rogue talents (canny observer, coax information — both *APG*), uncanny
snoop (*UI*); Extra Rogue Talent (*APG*). Page 241.

### 8. Mole — CR 5
Human bard 6, N. Deep-cover spy embedded in a noble's court, masters of disguise and
misdirection. Notable: bardic performance (fascinate, suggestion, inspire courage/competence),
enchantment/illusion spells (charm person, misdirection, detect thoughts, anonymous interaction
*ACG*, honeyed tongue *APG*); Steadfast Personality (*ACG*). Carries a *scroll of geomessage*
(new spell). Page 241.

### 9. Master Spy — CR 6
Human rogue (spy 7, *APG*), NE. The spider at the center of the guild's web of informants;
deciphers codes, plants false evidence, and coordinates agents. Notable: sneak attack +4d6,
poison use, skilled liar, multiple rogue talents (canny observer, coax information, hard to
fool, honeyed words, quick disguise — all *APG*), Disguise +18; buffs with *potion of heroism*.
Page 242.

### 10. Chemist — CR 7
Dwarf alchemist 8, NE. One of the guildmaster's two most trusted lieutenants; brews potions for
the guild and deadly poisons for silencing targets. Notable: fast bombs, sticky poison,
tanglefoot bomb (*UM*), poison use, mutagen; alchemical allocation (*APG*) to share potion
benefits; applies deathblade poison. Page 243.

### 11. Guildmaster — CR 8
Human rogue (chameleon 9, *UC*), NE. The hidden mastermind who moves incognito even among her
own forces, disguising herself as a lowly thief. Notable: sneak attack +5d6, effortless sneak,
favored terrain (urban), misdirection (12 stealth points), rogue talents (black market
connections *UC*, expert leaper *APG*, fast stealth, quick disguise *APG*, terrain mastery
*ACG*); circlet of persuasion and +1 shadow mithral chain shirt; buffs with *potion of heroism*
and *potion of cat's grace*. Page 244.
