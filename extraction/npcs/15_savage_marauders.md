# Savage Marauders — NPC Reference

Source: *Pathfinder RPG Villain Codex* pp. 174–185 (NPCs pp. 178–184).
This is reference documentation only (not loaded PCGen data). Reusable crunch
(rage powers, spells, magic item) from the group's *New Rules* is inventoried in
`extraction/crunch/15_savage_marauders.md`. Full numeric stat blocks are in the PDF
at the cited page; entries below summarise build, role, and notable mechanics.

New-rules elements used by these NPCs are marked `*` in the book: rage powers
*Erratic Charge*, *Furious Barrage*, *Furious Draw*, *Savage Hurl*, *Two-Fanged
Pounce*; spells *Dousing Rain*, *Reinvigorating Wind*, *Steady Saddle*, *Wicker
Horse*; magic item *Battle Strider's Boots*. (The New Rules also define the rage
power *Greater Erratic Charge*, which no printed NPC uses.)

## Entries

### 1. Frenzied Cutter — CR 2
Human barbarian (savage barbarian 3, *APG*), CN. The most numerous marauders; a
dual-wielding skirmisher who opens engagements with a devastating charge. Notable:
rage (9 rounds/day), naked courage, uncanny dodge; rage powers *Erratic Charge* and
*Two-Fanged Pounce* (both new); Double Slice and Two-Weapon Fighting with mwk kukris,
throwing axes. Drinks a *potion of mage armor* before combat. Page 178.

### 2. War Strider — CR 5
Human ranger 6, NE. Elite mobile skirmisher (Speed 45 ft. with *longstrider*) who
strikes from ambush with a +1 falchion, then retreats behind allies. Notable: combat
style (two-handed weapon, *APG*), favored enemy (animals, humans), Spring Attack,
Mobility; carries *Battle Strider's Boots* (new) as combat gear. Casts *longstrider*
pre-combat. Page 178.

### 3. Brutal Hurler — CR 3
Human barbarian 4, CN. Scout and thrown-weapon skirmisher who sneaks up on guards.
Notable: rage (11 rounds/day), trap sense, uncanny dodge; rage powers *Furious
Barrage*, *Furious Draw*, and *Savage Hurl* (all new); Deadly Aim and Point-Blank
Shot with mwk javelins and heavy mace. Page 179.

### 4. Wolf Arrow — CR 5
Half-elf ranger 6, CN. Swiftest and stealthiest marauder; rides a trained dire wolf
to scout and to attack rear-guard leaders and spellcasters. Notable: combat style
(archery), favored enemy (animals, humans), Improved Precise Shot, Rapid Shot,
Mounted Archery; +1 composite longbow. Casts ranger spells *ant haul* (*APG*) and
*Steady Saddle* (new) before combat. Page 179.

### 5. War Singer — CR 3
Human skald 4 (*ACG*), N. Battle-bard support who buffs with raging song. Notable:
raging song 11/day (inspired rage, song of marching), rage powers inspire ferocity
and reckless abandon (both *APG*); Skald spells known (glitterdust, hold person,
etc.); Arcane Strike, Combat Casting. Uses no new-rules elements from this book.
Page 180.

### 6. Rage Mage — CR 6
Human sorcerer 7, CE, elemental (water) bloodline. Blaster who converts energy spells
to cold via bloodline arcana. Notable: elemental ray SLA, Spell Focus/Greater Spell
Focus (evocation), *fireball*/*scorching ray*/*burning hands* (all recolored cold);
+1 earth breaker. Self-buffs with *bear's endurance*, *bull's strength*, *heroism*,
*mage armor*, *shield*. Uses no new-rules elements from this book. Page 180.

### 7. Spiked Charger — CR 5
Human barbarian 2/cavalier 4 (order of the star, *APG*), NE. Lance-and-barding cavalry
that shatters formations; also serves as negotiator/messenger. Notable: cavalier's
charge, challenge, rage (8 rounds/day), rage power ferocious mount (*APG*, mount rages
when rider does), tactician (Cavalry Formation, *UC*); Spirited Charge, Ride-By Attack.
Rides a warhorse (CR —; N Large animal, hp 57, bite + 2 hooves, evasion) statted on the
same page. Uses no new-rules elements from this book. Page 181.

### 8. Fiend-Born Rager — CR 7
Half-orc bloodrager (untouchable rager 8, *ACG*), CE, Large. Senior field commander
and hand-to-hand instructor who becomes a hulking, clawed monster. Notable: bloodrage
(20 rounds/day), claws (2 claws 3d6, bite), blood sanctuary, orc ferocity, DR 1/—,
energy resistance, SR 17; Improved Natural Attack (claw), Intimidating Prowess;
*furious* amulet of mighty fists (*UE*). Uses no new-rules elements from this book.
Page 182.

### 9. Mad Seeress — CR 7
Human skald (spell warrior 8, *ACG*), CN. The Crimson Lord's chief advisor and
second-in-command; a warrior-prophet who sows confusion and bolsters allies. Notable:
raging song 20/day (enhance weapons, song of marching, song of strength), spirit
totem, greater counterspell, lore master; +1 shock longspear. Skald spells known
include *Dousing Rain* and *Reinvigorating Wind* (both new), plus phantom steed,
confusion, gallant inspiration (*APG*), liberating command (*UC*), saving finale
(*APG*). Page 183.

### 10. Crimson Lord — CR 8
Human shaman 9 (*ACG*), CE, battle spirit / fiery soul. Warrior-shaman leader of the
tribe in both war and spirit matters. Notable: hexes (chant, flight, fortune,
misfortune), wandering hex (cinder dance), spirit magic and spirit-like abilities,
spirit animal (red fox); +2 greatsword. Prepared shaman spells include *Wicker Horse*
(new) alongside flame strike, wall of fire, fireball, greater magic weapon. Self-buffs
with barkskin, bull's strength, false life, greater magic weapon, magic vestment,
owl's wisdom. Page 184.
