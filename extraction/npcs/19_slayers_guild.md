# Slayers' Guild — NPC Reference

Source: *Pathfinder RPG Villain Codex* pp. 222–233 (NPCs pp. 226–232).
This is reference documentation only (not loaded PCGen data). Reusable crunch
(feats, magic items) from the group's *New Rules* (pp. 224–225) is inventoried in
`extraction/crunch/19_slayers_guild.md`. Full numeric stat blocks are in the PDF at
the cited page; entries below summarise build, role, and notable mechanics.

New-rules elements used by these NPCs are marked `*` in the book: feats *Deadly
Kiss*, *Two-Weapon Grace*, *Vishkanya Perfume*; magic items *Cestus of Security*,
*Clandestine Voucher*, *Ghungroos of Entrancement*, *Smoke Bomb of Forgetfulness*,
*Vishkanya Periapt*. The feat *Terrifying Assassination* is also new but is not used
by any NPC in this group.

## Entries

### 1. Sharpshooter — CR 6
Human slayer (sniper) 7 (*ACG*), NE. A guild sniper who makes the hit from range
when closing on a mark is undesirable. Uses the deadly sniper ability while prone
from a high vantage and rogue crawl to relocate between shots; studied target,
sneak attack +2d6, poison use, and blue whinnis on his bolts. Feats include Crossbow
Mastery^APG, Deadly Aim, Extra Slayer Talent^ACG (×3), Focused Shot^APG. Page 226.

### 2. Deadeye — CR 8
Human ninja 9 (*UC*), LE. Sniper who fights from cover or invisibility; sneak
attack +5d6, ki pool, ninja tricks (bleeding attack, deadly range^UC, snap shot^APG,
sniper's eye^APG, vanishing trick^UC), poison use. Carries a *Smoke Bomb of
Forgetfulness* to erase witnesses' memory of him after a failed hit. Page 226.

### 3. Murderer — CR 6
Human alchemist 7 (*APG*), NE. Poison crafter and bomb-thrower for the guild; bombs
11/day (4d6+4 fire, DC 17), Dexterity mutagen, discoveries (concentrate poison,
explosive missile^UC, poison conversion^UC, sticky poison). Also a two-weapon dagger
fighter with the *Two-Weapon Grace* feat (paired with Slashing Grace^ACG). Carries a
*Clandestine Voucher* holding 700 gp. Page 227.

### 4. Rutterkin — CR 7
Halfling rogue (poisoner) 8 (*APG*), NE. Master poisoner; poison use, sneak attack
+4d6, rogue talents (finesse rogue, hidden weapons^UC, lasting poison^APG,
underhanded^UC), and Sleight of Hand +26 to conceal a weapon. Carries deathblade and
Large scorpion venom. No new-rules elements. Page 227.

### 5. Skulk — CR 10
Half-orc rogue (skulking slayer) 11 (*ARG*), NE. Ambush brute deployed for direct
kills; sneak attack +6d6 plus 2 Str, bold strike, shifty, Improved Critical
(falchion), underhanded maneuvers (dirty trick, steal), pass for human. Feats
include Cleave/Great Cleave, Power Attack, Surprise Follow-Through^ARG. No new-rules
elements. Page 228.

### 6. Butcher — CR 8
Human slayer 9 (*ACG*), NE. Infiltrator who passes weeks in disguise (Disguise +22)
then strikes with paired handaxes; sneak attack +3d6, studied target, combat style
(two-weapon combat), slayer talents (poison use^ACG, ranger combat style^ACG ×2,
swift poison^APG). No new-rules elements. Page 229.

### 7. Operative — CR 9
Human witch 10 (*APG*), NE. Infiltrator-spellcaster with the deception patron;
prepared witch spells to CL 10th (baleful polymorph, dominate person, dimension
door), hexes (agony, cackle, charm, disguise, slumber, tongues), Split Hex^UM,
scorpion familiar. No new-rules elements. Page 229.

### 8. Zen Assassin — CR 11
Male human monk (zen archer) 6 / ninja 6 (*APG*, *UC*), LE. The guild's third-in-
command, a silent sniper who tests and executes recruits. Flurry of blows with a +1
human-bane composite longbow, perfect strike 7/day, sneak attack +3d6, ki archery/ki
arrows, ninja tricks (deadly range^UC ×2, flurry of stars^UC, snap shot^APG, sniper's
eye^APG, vanishing trick^UC); applies deathblade and purple worm poison. No new-rules
elements. Page 230.

### 9. Deadly Courtesan — CR 11
Female vishkanya rogue (deadly courtesan) 12 (*Bestiary 3*, *ARG*), NE. The guild's
second-in-command and a master of disguise (Disguise +31), poison, and seduction;
bardic performance, sneak attack +6d6, assassinate^UC, toxic 6/day, performance
strike^ARG. Uses the new feats *Deadly Kiss* and *Vishkanya Perfume* and wears the
*Ghungroos of Entrancement* and *Vishkanya Periapt*; applies sleep venom via poison
tattoos. Page 231.

### 10. Grandmother of Slayers — CR 12
Middle-aged half-elf slayer 7 / warpriest (cult leader) of Norgorber 6 (*ACG*), CE.
The guild matriarch and its top threat; sacred weapon, fervor 5/day, blessings (Evil,
Trickery), enthrall SLA, warpriest spells to CL 6th, sneak attack +4d6, studied
target, slayer talents. Wields a poisoned +1 human-bane gladius secured by the new
*Cestus of Security*, with Impaling Critical^UC and Slashing Grace^ACG. Page 232.
