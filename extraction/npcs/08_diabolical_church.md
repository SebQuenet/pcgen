# Diabolical Church — NPC Reference

Source: *Pathfinder RPG Villain Codex* pp. 90–101 (NPCs pp. 94–100).
This is reference documentation only (not loaded PCGen data). Reusable crunch from
the group's *New Rules* (pp. 92–93) is inventoried in
`extraction/crunch/08_diabolical_church.md`. Full numeric stat blocks are in the PDF
at the cited page; entries below summarise build, role, and notable mechanics.

New-rules elements used by these NPCs are marked `*` in the book: the archetype
*Hellcat* (Monk); magic items *Devil's Spit*, *Diabolus Bell*, *Eyes of Embersight*,
*Eyes of the Damned*, *Rod of Burning Blood*. (Devil's Spit and Rod of Burning Blood
are defined in the New Rules but not carried by any NPC in this group.)

Every NPC in this group is an Asmodean cultist and Lawful Evil.

## Entries

### 1. Diabolist — CR 6
Tiefling sorcerer 7 (infernal bloodline), LE Medium outsider (native). Blaster/face
of the "Infernal Zealots"; fire-heavy known spells (*fireball*, *scorching ray*,
*burning hands*, *flaming sphere*) plus enchantment (*charm person*, *suggestion*).
Bloodline: corrupting touch, beguiling liar, fiendish sorcery; resist cold/electricity/
fire 5; +2 vs. poison, +2 deflection vs. good. Feats include *Armor of the Pit* [ARG]
and *Fiend Sight* [ARG]. Gear: wand of darkness, headband of alluring charisma +2. Page 94.

### 2. Hellguard — CR 6
Human antipaladin (tyrant) 7 [APG, UI], LE. Front-line enforcer with aura of
cowardice; smite good, touch of corruption (3d6), cruelties (shaken, staggered),
fiendish boon (weapon +1), and slavery/tyranny variant channeling [UM] (negative
energy with channel penalty). Wields a +1 ranseur and repeating heavy crossbow;
immune to disease. Page 94.

### 3. Infiltrator — CR 7
Half-elf inquisitor (infiltrator) of Asmodeus 8 [APG, UM], LE. Spy/interrogator with
Torture inquisition [APG], judgment (3/day, 2 simultaneous), bane, solo tactics,
torturer's touch, necessary lies/misdirection, and +8 vs. lie-detection. Uses
*interrogation* [UM], *persuasive goad* [UM], *confess* [APG]. Feats include *Shield
Wall* / *Swap Places* [APG]. Gear: belt of incredible dexterity +2. Page 95.

### 4. Martinet — CR 7
Human monk (hellcat*) 8, LE. Grappling/torturer bruiser built on the new **Hellcat
(Monk)** archetype: hellcat fury (slashing unarmed strikes, bleed on crit), torture
training (extra saves vs. conditions), crucible of pain (DR 4/nonlethal), hellcat ki
(10-point pool, cold iron/magic/silver/hellcat ki). Flurries with a +1 tiger fork
[UC]; Improved Grapple, Stunning Fist. Gear: cruel amulet of mighty fists [UE]. Page 95.

### 5. Hellpriest — CR 8
Human warpriest of Asmodeus 9 [ACG], LE. Martial priest: blessings (Evil, Fire),
channel negative energy (3d6), fervor, sacred weapon/sacred armor, prepared cleric
spells including *vision of Hell* [UM] and *bestow curse*. Wields a +1 flaming
ranseur. Page 96.

### 6. Firebrand — CR 9
Half-elf bard (demagogue) 10 [UM], LE. Demagogue/rabble-rouser: bardic performance
with incite violence and inspire greatness; enchantment-focused known spells
(*dominate person*, *charm monster*, *lesser geas*, *denounce* [APG], *malicious
spite* [UM]). Greater Spell Focus (enchantment), famous. Carries a **diabolus bell***
(divine focus + ward the faithful / doom / death knell area effect) and headband of
alluring charisma +2. Page 96.

### 7. Eliminator — CR 9
Human slayer 10 [ACG], LE. Two-weapon / archery assassin: sneak attack +3d6, studied
target, slayer talents (poison use [ACG], ranger combat styles), stalker. Uses blue
whinnis, purple worm poison; gear +2 agile breastplate [APG], +1 daggers, mwk
composite shortbow. No new-rules item. Page 97.

### 8. Infernalist — CR 10
Human cleric of Asmodeus 11 (Fire + Evil [Devil subdomain, APG]), LE. Battle-cleric:
channel negative energy (6d6), hell's corruption, scythe of evil, fire bolt domain
power, resist fire 10, prepared spells to 6th (*planar binding* devils only,
*flame strike*, *unholy blight*). Wears **eyes of the damned*** (gaze attack →
*vision of Hell* [UM] phantasm). Page 97.

### 9. Knight Tyrant — CR 11
Human antipaladin (tyrant) 12 [APG, UI], LE. Cruel martial leader and head of the
church's forces: auras of cowardice/despair/vengeance, smite good, touch of
corruption (6d6), cruelties (frightened/shaken/staggered/stunned), fiendish boon
(fiendish servant — advanced erinyes, 3/day), slavery/tyranny variant channeling
[UM], dirty-trick feats. Wears **eyes of embersight*** (darkvision / charge devil's
spit). Companions: Fiendish Servant (advanced erinyes, CR —) and Dark Destrier
(cauchemar mount, CR 11). Page 98.

### 10. Grand Inquisitor — CR 11
Human inquisitor (sanctified slayer) of Asmodeus 12 [APG, ACG], LE. Assassin-priest
head of the inquisition: Law inquisition (Devil subdomain [APG]), greater bane,
hell's corruption, scythe of evil, sneak attack +3d6, studied target, stalwart,
DR 10/adamantine, resist fire 30; feint-focused feats (*Lookout*/*Outflank*/*Precise
Strike* [APG]), poison use [ACG]. Uses *planeslayer's call* [ACG], deathblade, blue
whinnis. Activates **eyes of embersight*** in combat. Page 99.

### 11. Hierarch — CR 12
Human cleric of Asmodeus 13, LE. Church leader; legal/political manipulator with
Conversion inquisition [UM] + Evil (Devil subdomain [APG]) domains, hell's
corruption, scythe of evil, slavery/tyranny variant channeling [UM], swaying word.
High-level prepared spells (*blasphemy*, *destruction*, *harm*, *planar binding*
devils only, *summon monster VI*). Enchantment-focused; Persuasive, Greater Spell
Focus (enchantment). Gear: +2 full plate, headband of mental prowess +2. No new-rules
item. Page 100.

## Encounters
Book pp. 100–101 assemble these NPCs into four ready encounters:
- **The Sudden Strike (CR 12)** — Diabolist, Hellguards (2), Hellpriest, Martinets (2).
- **The Forked Tongue (CR 13)** — Firebrand, Infernalist, Infiltrators (2), Martinets (2).
- **The Harsh Truth (CR 16)** — Diabolists (2), Eliminators (2), Hellpriests (4),
  Hierarch, Infernalists (2). (The hierarch's traveling coterie.)
