## Entries

### 1. Guard Spotter — CR 1
Half-elf fighter 2, LN. Front-line lookout who calls out and marks fleeing
suspects. Notable: high Perception, works in teams; typically has the *Coordinated
Capture* teamwork feat to lock down foes with allies. Page 46.

### 2. Guard Sniper — CR 2
Human ranger 3, LN. Ranged specialist covering escape routes; favored enemy
(humanoid) and Stealth. Uses *Escape Alarm* wards on patrol perimeters. Page 46.

### 3. Shield Warden — CR 2
Dwarf fighter (shielded fighter 3, *APG*), LN. Defensive anchor with shield
maneuvers; holds chokepoints during arrests. Page 47.

### 4. Guard Mage — CR 3
Human evoker 4, LN. Battlefield control and detection; carries *Escape Alarm* and
often a *Badge of Authority* to command bystanders. Page 47.

### 5. Misguided Paladin — CR 2
Human paladin 3, LG. A genuinely well-meaning holy warrior manipulated into
serving the corrupt guard; smite evil, detect evil at will, lay on hands. Useful as
a redeemable or conflicted opponent. Page 48.

### 6. Reformed Criminal — CR 3
Human rogue 4, LN. Ex-thief turned informant/thief-taker; trapfinding, sneak
attack, and street knowledge. May carry an *Erasing Book*. Page 48.

### 7. Guard Healer — CR 1
Human cleric of Abadar 2, LN. Field medic and morale support; channel energy,
cure spells. Page 49.

### 8. Interrogator — CR 4
Human inquisitor of Abadar 5 (*APG*), LN. Extracts confessions through judgment,
Intimidate, and detection magic; frequently deploys *Pliability Elixir* and *Beacon
of Guilt* to break suspects. Page 50.

### 9. Persuader — CR 3
Human bard 4, LN. Social manipulator using bardic performance and enchantments to
"coax" cooperation; augments natural persuasion with spells and a *Pliability
Elixir*. Page 50.

### 10. Jailor — CR 4
Human fighter 5, LN. Warden of the cells; grappling and lockdown focus, carries a
*Guard's Kit* and manacles. Page 51.

### 11. Chief Detective — CR 5
Human investigator 6 (*ACG*), LN. Lead investigator with inspiration, alchemy, and
broad Knowledge/senses; verifies documents with a *Quill of Verification* and hides
evidence with *Alchemical Cleaner*. Page 51.

### 12. Guard Captain — CR 6
Human fighter 7, LN. Commands the watch; strong martial build with leadership and
teamwork feats (*Coordinated Capture*), directing the group's tactics. Page 52.
