# Cruel Musketeers — NPC Reference

Source: *Pathfinder RPG Villain Codex* pp. 54–65 (NPCs pp. 58–64).
This is reference documentation only (not loaded PCGen data). Reusable crunch
(feats, firearms, magic items) from the group's *New Rules* lives in the
`villain_codex` source LST files. Full numeric stat blocks are in the PDF at the
cited page; entries below summarise build, role, and notable mechanics.

New-rules elements used by these NPCs are marked `*` in the book and are provided
as loadable data: feats *Covering Fire*, *Musketeer's Daring*, *Musketeer's
Dodge*, *Musketeer's Sidestep*, *Protective Line*; firearms *Dragoon Musket*,
*Dragoon Pistol*, *Dragoon Cartridge*; magic items *Mage Shot (Acid/Cold/
Electricity/Fire)*. (Feats *Musketeer's Reposition* and item *Dragoon Pistol* are
defined in New Rules but not used by any NPC in this group.)

## Entries

### 1. Oddfellow — CR 2
Half-orc fighter 3, CN. Rank-and-file bruiser wielding a musket modified with a
battleaxe (axe musket, *UC*); Amateur Gunslinger gives him deeds (quick clear)
and grit (1). Takes a shot or two before rushing into melee. No new-rules
elements. Page 58.

### 2. Little Devil — CR 4
Halfling gunslinger 5 (*UC*), CG. Small sniper who hides and picks off foes from
a distance; deeds (deadeye, gunslinger's dodge, pistol-whip, utility shot),
nimble +1, musket with paper alchemical cartridges (*UC*). No new-rules elements.
Page 58.

### 3. Pistoleer — CR 3
Human swashbuckler (picaroon 4, *ACG*), N. Rapier-and-pistol duelist; panache (4),
charmed life, derring-do/dodging panache/precise strike deeds, mwk double-barreled
pistol (*UC*). Buffs with a *potion of shield of faith* before combat. No
new-rules elements. Page 59.

### 4. Sneak Shot — CR 4
Half-elf rogue 5, NE. Stealthy scout/assassin; sneak attack +3d6, evasion,
uncanny dodge, rogue talents firearm training and grit (*UC*), pistol (*UC*).
Ambushes surprised foes. No new-rules elements. Page 59.

### 5. Holy Shooter — CR 4
Human inquisitor of Torag 5 (*APG*), LN. Firearm-toting divine support with the
Black Powder inquisition (*UC*); judgment, bane, disruptive shot, cure spells.
Notable: has the *Protective Line* teamwork feat and carries *Mage Shot
(electricity)* ×2 and *Mage Shot (fire)* ×2 for his mwk musket. Page 60.

### 6. Arcane Musketeer — CR 5
Elf wizard (spellslinger 6, *UC*), LN. Flying blaster who casts *mage armor* and
*fly*, then rains fireball/lightning bolt and firearm shots; arcane gun, mage
bullets. Carries *Mage Shot (electricity)* ×2 and *Mage Shot (fire)* ×2 with a
+1 musket. Page 60.

### 7. Dragoon Marshal — CR 5
Dwarf fighter (tactician 6, *UC*), LE. Leads firing lines; tactician 1/day grants
allies the *Covering Fire* teamwork feat. Wields a *+1 dragoon musket* fed by
*Dragoon Cartridges* ×4, and has both *Covering Fire* and *Protective Line* feats.
Page 61.

### 8. Black-Powder Sage — CR 6
Human investigator (steel hound 7, *ACG*), N. Gadgeteer with studied combat/strike,
extracts, inspiration, packing heat, and the *Musketeer's Dodge* feat. Fields a
*+1 dragoon musket* with *Dragoon Cartridges* ×5 and the full set of dragoon-
cartridge *Mage Shots* (acid, cold, electricity, fire). Page 61.

### 9. Bold Captain — CR 6
Gnome swashbuckler (mouser, musketeer 7, *ACG*), CN. Flamboyant leader; panache
(5), charmed life, many deeds, gnome spell-like abilities. Notable feats
*Musketeer's Daring* and *Musketeer's Sidestep*; supports the firing line with a
mwk musket before entering melee with a +1 rapier. Page 62.

### 10. Grim Captain — CR 7
Human gunslinger (musket master 8, *UC*), NE. Ruthless firearm specialist; deeds
(dead shot, fast musket, targeting, startling shot), grit (4), musket training,
+1 musket. No new-rules elements (all feats from other sources). Page 62.

### 11. Baroness — CR 7
Half-elf magus 8 (*UM*), LN. Ambitious spellblade ally of the lord marshal;
arcane pool, spell combat, spellstrike, magus arcana, prepared spells to 3rd
(fireball, haste). Wields a +1 scimitar and mwk double-barreled pistol (*UC*). No
new-rules elements. Page 63.

### 12. Lord Marshal — CR 8
Human cavalier (musketeer 9, *APG*/*UC*), LN. The group's brooding leader; banner,
challenge, order of the cockatrice, and greater tactician 2/day granting allies
*Covering Fire* or *Protective Line*. Carries *Covering Fire* and *Protective
Line* feats, a *+1 dragoon musket*, and *Dragoon Cartridges* ×3. Page 64.
