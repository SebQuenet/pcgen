# Arcane Society — NPC Reference

Source: *Pathfinder RPG Villain Codex* pp. 6–17 (NPCs pp. 10–16).
This is reference documentation only (not loaded PCGen data). Reusable crunch
(spells, occult rituals) from the group's *New Rules* lives in the crunch
inventory (`extraction/crunch/01_arcane_society.md`). Full numeric stat blocks are
in the PDF at the cited page; entries below summarise build, role, and notable
mechanics.

New-rules elements used by these NPCs are marked `*` in the book and are provided
as loadable data: spells *Amnesia*, *Appearance of Life (Greater)*, *Covetous
Urge*; occult rituals *Blush of Youth*, *Unwavering Vigilance*. Superscript
references (`APG`, `UM`, `UC`, `OA`) point to elements from other books, cited but
not defined here.

## Entries

### 1. Corrupt Scrivener — CR 8
Half-orc diviner (foresight, *APG*) 9, NE. Scholar and archivist who gathers,
stores, and quietly hoards the society's secrets and member dossiers. Notable:
see invisibility, foretell (9 rounds/day), forewarned, prescience (7/day); arcane
bond familiar (greensting scorpion, *UM*); opposition schools enchantment and
illusion. Retiring personality — teleports away with incriminating documents in
the surprise round rather than fight. No new-rules elements in his stat block.
Page 10.

### 2. Wicked Orator — CR 10
Human bard 11, LE. Propagandist who spreads tales of the society's benevolence.
Notable: bardic performance 28 rounds/day (inspire courage +3, inspire greatness,
suggestion, fascinate), versatile performances, Lingering Performance, Arcane
Strike, ranged +3 shortbow attacker. No new-rules elements. Page 11.

### 3. Spellmaster — CR 11
Human magus 12 (*UM*), LE. Lead bureaucrat and blade-mage handling daily affairs
and recruitment. Notable: arcane pool (+3, 10 points), spellstrike, improved spell
combat, magus arcana (arcane accuracy, close range, critical strike, hasted
assault — all *UM*); DR 10/adamantine from stoneskin; +2 rapier with Improved
Critical. No new-rules elements. Page 12.

### 4. First Advisor — CR 12
Halfling witch 13 (*APG*), NE. The Spellqueen's scout, spy, and diviner-confidante;
prefers to influence events from behind the scenes. Notable: hexes (slumber, evil
eye, misfortune, cackle, ice tomb, flight, etc. — *APG*/*UM*), bat familiar, patron
portents (*UM*); high-level divinations (true seeing, discern lies, contact other
plane). Avoids combat. No new-rules elements. Page 13.

### 5. High Enchanter — CR 12
Half-elf enchanter 13, CE. Mind-controller who uses people as playthings. Notable:
aura of despair, dazing touch SLA, enchanting smile, contingency (dimension door);
opposition schools conjuration and evocation. Uses new-rules spells *Amnesia* (4th)
and *Covetous Urge* (2nd) alongside dominate person, mass suggestion, and geas.
Page 14.

### 6. Grand Caller — CR 14
Human conjurer (teleportation, *APG*) 15, CE. Summoner of extraplanar minions; bound
to a marilith (via True Name) and corrupted by a succubus's profane gift. Notable:
shift ability, Augment Summoning, Superior Summoning, Spell Perfection (aqueous
orb), Dazing/Persistent metamagic; contingency (teleport), immune to dimensional
anchor and fear; opposition schools evocation and necromancy. Per the New Rules
intro, he (with the Spellqueen) developed the group's rituals. No new-rules spells
in his prepared list. Page 15.

### 7. The Eminent Spellqueen — CR 14
Human lich sorcerer 12 (*Bestiary* 188), NE. The society's leader, a lich disguised
as a beautiful philanthropist, who murders youths to fuel blood-price magic.
Notable: paralyzing touch, fear aura, rejuvenation, DR 15/bludgeoning and magic,
undead immunities; arcane bloodline, metamagic adept, Persistent/Quicken Spell.
Uses new-rules spells *Amnesia* (4th) and *Appearance of Life, Greater* (5th), and
keeps the new-rules ritual *Blush of Youth* active (an SQ entry) to wear a youthful
living disguise. Page 16.
