# Nature's Scourge — NPC Reference

Source: *Pathfinder RPG Villain Codex* pp. 138–149 (New Rules pp. 140–141; NPCs pp. 142–148; Encounters p. 149).
This is reference documentation only (not loaded PCGen data). Reusable crunch
(rage powers, feat, spell, occult rituals, magic items) from the group's *New Rules*
is inventoried in `extraction/crunch/12_natures_scourge.md`. Full numeric stat blocks
are in the PDF at the cited page; entries below summarise build, role, and notable
mechanics.

New-rules elements used by these NPCs are marked `*` in the book: rage powers
*Atavism Totem* / *Atavism Totem, Lesser* / *Atavism Totem, Greater*; feat *Nature's
Wrath*; spell *Burning Entanglement*; occult rituals *Atavistic Reversion*, *Natural
Reclamation*; magic items *Fungal Slippers*, *Necklace of Stolen Breath*.

## Entries

### 1. Human Hunter — CR 5
Human ranger 6, LE. Two-weapon dagger fighter and man-hunter; favored enemy (humans
+4, native outsiders +2), combat style (two-weapon combat). Prepares *feather step*
[APG] and *hunter's howl* [APG]. No new-rules elements. Page 142.

### 2. Wild Rager — CR 6
Half-orc barbarian 7, CE. Greatsword berserker; rage 18 rounds/day with rage powers
intimidating glare, no escape, and superstition +3. Uses only published rage powers
(no new-rules totem here). Page 142.

### 3. Wildfire — CR 4
Human druid (nature fang 5, *ACG*), NE. Fire-themed druid with slayer talents (studied
target, sneak attack +1d6) and the Fire domain. Prepares the new spell *Burning
Entanglement* (listed as "burn. entangle.*" at 3rd level, DC 18). Page 143.

### 4. Immolator — CR 5
Human witch 6 (*APG*), CE. Flying pyromancer with hexes (blight, cackle, evil eye,
flight, slumber). Prepares the new spell *Burning Entanglement* (3rd, DC 18) alongside
*fireball*. Familiar: greensting scorpion. Page 143.

### 5. Renewer — CR 7
Human shaman 8 (*ACG*), NE. Recruitment officer with a rebuild-nature philosophy;
life/nature spirit, wandering hex (life sight), Augment Summoning and summon spells.
Prepares the new spell *Burning Entanglement* (3rd, DC 18) as her wide-area hamper.
Uses Flexible Hex [ACG] to swap life sight for life link. Page 144.

### 6. Tracker — CR 6
Elf hunter 7 (*ACG*), NE. Ranged bounty hunter with a tiger animal companion; animal
focus, hunter tactics, teamwork feats (Coordinated Shot [ACG], Lookout [APG]). No
new-rules elements. Page 145.

### 7. Tiger — CR —
N Large animal, the Tracker's animal companion. Pounce, rake, grab; bite +10 and 2
claws +11 (3d6+7). Stat block is partially cut off in the source (hp 45, feats and
tricks listed). No new-rules elements. Page 145.

### 8. Wrathbringer — CR 8
Fungus leshy skald 7 (*B3* 177, *ACG* 49), NE. A fungus leshy the Blight Caller
animated by merging a nature spirit with a piece of his own rage. Uses the new rage
powers *Atavism Totem* and *Atavism Totem, Lesser* (via skald raging song), the new
feat *Nature's Wrath*, and wears *Fungal Slippers*. Inspired-rage buffer with
good hope / haste / gallant inspiration. Page 146.

### 9. Bog's Embrace — CR 10
Bog nixie sorcerer 8 (*B3* 201), NE. Sadistic fey manipulator, the Blight Caller's
closest thing to a second. Fey bloodline, enchantment/illusion controller (confusion,
deep slumber, suggestion). Wears a *Necklace of Stolen Breath* (2 minor soul bones and
1 soul bone) that she crafts herself from the bones of those she drowns. Page 147.

### 10. Blight Caller — CR 10
Human druid (blight druid 11, *APG* 98), NE. Founder and leader of Nature's Scourge;
usually fights wild-shaped as a behemoth hippopotamus (bite 8d8+12 plus grab, trample).
Destruction domain, destructive aura/smite, miasma. He developed the *Nature's Wrath*
feat and collected the group's occult rituals (*Atavistic Reversion*, *Natural
Reclamation*), and created the Wrathbringer. Page 148.

## Encounter notes
- **Wrath of the Plants (CR 12)**: three treants (*Bestiary* 266) each replace
  Improved Sunder with the new feat *Nature's Wrath*. Page 149.
- Other encounters (Ambush Predators CR 8, Renewal by Destruction CR 12, Nature's
  Vengeance CR 13) reuse the stat blocks above plus a deinonychus, giant tarantulas,
  and treants from published bestiaries. Page 149.
