# Ruthless Brigands — NPC Reference

Source: *Pathfinder RPG Villain Codex* pp. 162–173 (New Rules pp. 164–165; NPCs pp. 166–172).
This is reference documentation only (not loaded PCGen data). Reusable crunch
(archetype, spells, magic items) from the group's *New Rules* is inventoried in the
companion crunch file. Full numeric stat blocks are in the PDF at the cited page;
entries below summarise build, role, and notable mechanics.

New-rules elements marked `*` in the book: archetype *Seasoned Commander* (fighter);
spells *Nature's Paths*, *Sand Table*; magic items *Commander's Tent*, *Moss Cloak*,
*Satchel of Plentiful Feed*. Only the Mercenary Captain uses any of them directly
(she is a *Seasoned Commander* fighter and carries a *Commander's Tent*).

## Entries

### 1. Witty Jury-Rigger — CR 1
Human investigator (empiricist 2, *ACG*), NG. Camp mechanic/fixer who keeps gear in
working order. Notable: alchemy (alchemy crafting +2), ceaseless observation,
inspiration (4/day), trapfinding +1; prepared extracts (*crafter's fortune*,
*shield*, *stone fist*); pre-buffs with a *potion of cat's grace* (base statistics
without it are given). Page 167.

### 2. Crusty Cook — CR 2
Human fighter 2/rogue 1, CN. Cook/field medic who fights and tends wounds in a
pinch. Notable: sneak attack +1d6, trapfinding +1; carries a healer's kit and cure
light wounds potions; mwk spear with Weapon Focus. Page 166.

### 3. Seasoned Forager — CR 2
Human hunter 3 (*ACG*), N. Scout who ranges ahead with his mountain lion companion.
Notable: hunter spells known (CL 3rd), animal companion (mountain lion), animal
focus (3 minutes/day), hunter tactics, track +1, wild empathy +4; archery focus
(mwk longbow, Point-Blank/Precise Shot, Coordinated Shot); drinks a *potion of
cat's grace* (base statistics without it are given). Page 168.

### 4. Bawdy Joker — CR 2
Human bard 3, CN. Morale/social support and light enchanter. Notable: bardic
performance 10 rounds/day (countersong, distraction, fascinate, inspire competence
+2, inspire courage +1); bard spells known (CL 3rd) incl. *charm person*, *hideous
laughter*; versatile performance (comedy); Lingering Performance, Spellsong. Page 169.

### 5. Cunning Quartermaster — CR 3
Human rogue 4, N. Scavenger/supplier who charms and wheedles supplies out of anyone.
Notable: sneak attack +2d6, evasion, trap sense +1, uncanny dodge, trapfinding +2;
four rogue talents (black market connections, charmer, hard to fool, survivalist);
Extra Rogue Talent (×2); broad social skills and seven languages. Page 166.

### 6. Bombastic Engineer — CR 3
Dwarf fighter 4, NE. Loudmouth siege/crossbow specialist who fights at range.
Notable: hatred, armor training 1, dwarven defensive training; Combat Expertise,
Coordinated Defense, Defensive Combat Training, Rapid Reload, Weapon Focus (heavy
crossbow); high Craft/Knowledge (engineering). Page 167.

### 7. Grizzled Healer — CR 4
Human warpriest of Gorum 5 (*ACG*), CN. Front-line healer and buffer. Notable:
blessings 5/day (Destruction, War), channel positive energy 3/day, fervor 6/day,
sacred weapon (1d8, +1, 5 rounds/day); warpriest spells prepared (CL 5th) incl.
*cure moderate wounds*, *delay poison*; deathwatch always active; +1 greatsword. Page 169.

### 8. Harsh Lieutenant — CR 5
Human fighter (tactician 6, *UC*), LN. Disciplinarian second-in-command who
coordinates the soldiers. Notable: tactician 2/day (6 rounds), armor training 1;
Coordinated Maneuvers, Practiced Tactician, Intimidating Prowess; +1 longsword,
+1 breastplate. Page 170.

### 9. Arcane Artillerist — CR 5
Halfling evoker 6, NE. Mobile artillery mage who stays on the battlefield's edge.
Notable: intense spells (+3 damage), force missile 6/day, arcane bond (wand of
scorching ray); opposition schools divination and necromancy; evoker spells prepared
(CL 6th) incl. *fireball*, *lightning bolt*, *scorching ray*; Craft Wand, Elemental
Focus; pre-casts *mage armor* (base statistics without it given). Page 171.

### 10. Mercenary Captain — CR 6
Human fighter (**seasoned commander***) 7, LE. Charismatic leader who fuses veteran
combat with battlefield tactics. Notable: **inspiring speech +1 1/day (9 rounds)**
and **tactician 2/day (6 rounds)** from the *Seasoned Commander* archetype; armor
training 2; Coordinated Defense, Coordinated Maneuvers, Outflank, Pack Attack,
Flanking Foil; +1 longsword, +1 breastplate, cloak of resistance +1, and a
***Commander's Tent***; drinks a *potion of bull's strength* (base statistics
without it given). Page 172.
