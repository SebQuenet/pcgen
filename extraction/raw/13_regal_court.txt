      crop. Power and vitality are its commerce. Life is brimming with adventure

      of it lies rotting in the ditch. Time robs vitality from us all, especially those who
      do nothing to defend themselves against the inevitable. The empire crumbles,

      the subversive elements that have latched on and will not let go.”
